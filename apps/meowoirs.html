Features Built for quiet memories

Everything you need, nothing extra

Simple tools for keeping every photo, quirk, and mood reflection organized by cat

Dedicated cat profiles (home and street)

Organize profiles for indoor companions and neighborhood street cats. Keep photos, notes, and individual memory timelines distinct for each cat.

Daily photo and note journaling

Save photos alongside your daily notes. Record the quiet naps in sunlit spots, sudden bursts of playfulness, and familiar routines.

Dual mood reflections

Capture feelings on both sides of every memory: record how your cat seemed alongside your own reflection to remember how the day felt.

100% offline-first and private

Your memories belong to you alone. No account is required and no remote servers collect your photos — everything stays in local Room SQLite storage.
