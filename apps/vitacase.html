Features Four dedicated modules

Everything you need, nothing extra

VitaCase stays focused on keeping your medical documents, medication schedule, and doctor contacts organized locally

PDF and blood test document vault

Save blood test PDFs, lab printouts, imaging reports, and prescriptions directly in an encrypted local folder so every document is ready before an appointment — stored as original files with no OCR text extraction.

Medication reminders and stock

Set clear daily medication and supplement schedules with local alerts and remaining-dose counts so you know when to take a dose or refill a prescription.

Dedicated saved doctors page

Keep your doctors organized on their own directory page with specialty, hospital or clinic name, phone number, and personal visit notes for quick lookup.

Private lock and emergency details

Files stay inside hardware-backed Android Keystore encryption, protected by an optional 6-digit PIN and biometric authentication alongside blood type and emergency contacts.
