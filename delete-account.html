Data deletion instructions

All health documents, medication schedules, and saved doctor records in VitaCase live directly on your Android device — never on remote Limanex servers.

Local storage: no remote cloud accounts

VitaCase operates without user accounts, usernames, or Limanex-hosted cloud databases. PDFs, blood test reports, medication schedules, and saved doctor entries are stored in your device's encrypted local vault using AES-256-GCM and hardware-backed key storage.

How to delete your data

01

In-app complete data wipe ("Clear All Data" / "Tüm Verileri Temizle")

Open VitaCase and navigate to Settings → Data & Security ("Veri ve Güvenlik") → tap "Clear All Data". This immediately deletes the local database, encrypted vault files, FlutterSecureStorage encryption key, saved doctors, and scheduled local reminders.

02

Application uninstallation

Uninstalling VitaCase removes the isolated application sandbox and all stored local files on your device. If your device has Google Drive / Android Auto Backup enabled at the OS level, manage OS backups through your Google Account device backup settings.

03

Selective manual deletion

Open or swipe any individual PDF document, medication entry, doctor record, or appointment inside the app and tap "Delete" to remove only that item permanently.

Diagnostic telemetry and privacy inquiries

While your vault documents never leave your device, VitaCase uses third-party SDKs (Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, and Sentry) for anonymized crash diagnostics and app stability metrics.

To submit a formal GDPR or KVKK deletion inquiry regarding anonymized diagnostic telemetry, email limanexsoftware@outlook.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request — VitaCase.
