Legal and Veterinary Health Disclaimer

Effective Date: September 7, 2026 · Legal Version: 1.0 · Operator: LIMANEX
Crucial notice: not a veterinary or medical service

Meowoirs is strictly a personal memory journal. If your cat shows signs of illness, injury, lethargy, vomiting, or breathing difficulties, contact a licensed veterinarian immediately.

1. Nature of the Application

Meowoirs is a recreational, emotional, and personal memorialization diary built for cat guardians and admirers.

2. Not a Veterinary or Medical Service

The Application does not provide:

  • Medical or veterinary diagnosis
  • Treatments, prescriptions, or clinical dosing plans
  • Emergency triage or symptom assessment
  • Clinical behavioral or dietary advice

3. Subjective Mood and Behavior Tracking Disclaimer

Mood labels in Meowoirs ("playful", "sleepy", "grumpy", "affectionate", "curious", "calm", and "energetic") are informal storytelling prompts, not clinical, psychometric, or veterinary scales. Watch closely for warning signs such as sudden lethargy, vomiting, appetite loss, or breathing difficulty and consult a veterinarian promptly.

4. No Warranty and User Assumption of Risk

As a pet guardian or caregiver, you retain sole responsibility for monitoring animal health, nutrition, vaccination schedules, and safety.

5. Emergency Contact Reminder

Keep phone numbers readily accessible outside any mobile app for your local veterinarian, 24-hour emergency animal hospital, poison control hotline, and municipal animal welfare services.

6. Notice Regarding This Documentation

This page provides descriptive product documentation and does not constitute individualized legal advice.
