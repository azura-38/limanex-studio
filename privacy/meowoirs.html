Meowoirs Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 7, 2026 · Legal Version: 1.0 · Operator: LIMANEX
Privacy by Design: 100% Local-First Architecture

Your cat profiles, photos, notes, and mood reflections exist solely on your physical Android device. Meowoirs operates without remote Limanex servers or cloud synchronization.

1. Introduction and Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how LIMANEX handles data for the Android mobile application Meowoirs (com.limanex.meowoirs), a personal, local-first cat memory journal and relationship diary.

2. Operator Information

Developer / Operator: LIMANEX · Jurisdiction: Republic of Türkiye · Email: limanexsoftware@outlook.com · Website: https://limanexstudio.com

3. Definitions

"Application" refers to Meowoirs; "User" means the person using the Application on their "Device"; "User Content" encompasses all profiles, notes, mood reflections, and photo URIs entered by you; "Local Storage" means AndroidX Room SQLite and Jetpack DataStore on your device; "Third-Party Services" means Google AdMob, Google UMP, and Firebase Crashlytics.

4. What Meowoirs Stores Locally on Your Device

  • Cat Profiles: Name, Home or Street classification, local photo URI, optional note, and timestamp.
  • Memories and Notes: Cat ID and name, memory text note, photo content URI, cat mood reflection, user emotional reflection, and timestamp.
  • App Preferences: Optional local nickname, avatar, favorite cat ID, onboarding state, and appearance settings stored in Jetpack DataStore.

5. Information We Do Not Collect

  • No user accounts, usernames, or passwords
  • No payment cards or banking information
  • No address book or contact list access
  • No audio, microphone, or background camera sensor access
  • No GPS coordinates, background location, or clinical veterinary records

6. Photo Privacy and Android Photo Picker

Meowoirs uses Android's privacy-preserving Photo Picker (ActivityResultContracts.PickVisualMedia). It requests no broad media permissions (no READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, or CAMERA permissions). Selected images use isolated content URIs and are never uploaded to remote clouds.

7. Data Deletion and Retention Architecture

Deleting an entry immediately and permanently removes it from the local Room SQLite database. Deleting a cat profile does not automatically delete existing journal memories, though it clears Favorite Cat references. Clearing Android app data or uninstalling Meowoirs permanently erases the local SQLite database (subject to OS-level Google Account backup settings managed by you).

8. Third-Party Services (AdMob, UMP, Crashlytics)

Third-party SDKs operate in strict isolation from your journal entries and photos:

  • A. Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Displays Banner (ca-app-pub-587701117866406/2930394553) and Interstitial (ca-app-pub-587701117866406/2195897382) ads under App ID ca-app-pub-587701117866406~4355156867. Meowoirs includes no rewarded video, paid subscriptions, or in-app purchases.
  • B. Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK: Manages EEA/UK GDPR and IAB TCF v2.2 ad consent. Consent choices can be updated via Settings → "Gizlilik Tercihleri" (Privacy Options) or Android Advertising ID settings.
  • C. Firebase Crashlytics: Captures technical crash stack traces, device model, OS version, build identifier, and memory state to diagnose crashes without reading journal text or photos. See Google Privacy Policy.

9. Data Retention

Local User Content stays on your device solely until you delete it or uninstall the Application. Retention of diagnostic or ad-delivery logs by Google/Firebase follows Google's published retention schedules.

10. Cookie Policy Statement

The Meowoirs mobile application uses no web cookies. The Limanex Studio static website sets no marketing cookies, tracking pixels, or web analytics cookies.

11. Applicable Privacy Rights (KVKK, GDPR, CCPA)

You maintain complete custody over local records under Turkish Law No. 6698 (KVKK), EU/UK GDPR, and CCPA/CPRA. LIMANEX does not sell personal information.

12. Children's Privacy

Meowoirs is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable digital consent threshold in your region).

13. Security Practices and Limitations

Data relies on standard Android application sandboxing on non-rooted devices. LIMANEX explicitly avoids exaggerated "military-grade" marketing claims and recommends maintaining device-level screen locks.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Updates to this policy are reflected by updating the Effective Date above and publishing the revised version at /privacy/meowoirs.
