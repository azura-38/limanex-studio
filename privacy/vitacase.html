VitaCase Terms and Privacy

Version 1.1.7-2026-10 · Last updated: October 11, 2026 · Operator: LIMANEX
Local custody and no medical advice

VitaCase is a personal health organization tool that encrypts documents, medication schedules, and saved doctor entries locally on your device using AES-256-GCM. It does not perform OCR text extraction or automated lab interpretation, and does not provide medical advice.

Section 1 — Terms of Use

1.1 Binding Agreement

By downloading, installing, or using VitaCase ("the Application"), developed and operated by LIMANEX ("we", "our", or "Operator"), you agree to these Terms of Use. If you do not agree, do not use the Application.

1.2 Service Description

VitaCase is an offline-first personal health organizer designed for storing personal health files and schedules locally:

  • Encrypted Document Vault: Store blood test PDFs, lab printouts, imaging summaries, and prescription files locally in AES-256-GCM encrypted storage. Files are stored as uploaded documents without optical character recognition (OCR) or automated data extraction.
  • Medication & Stock Tracking: Schedule daily medication reminders and keep track of remaining tablet or capsule inventory locally.
  • Doctor Directory & Appointments: Maintain a dedicated saved doctors page with physician names, specialties, clinics, phone numbers, and upcoming appointment dates.
  • Health & Critical Info: Optionally record personal physical metrics (such as height, weight, BMI, and vitals) alongside blood type and emergency contact details.

1.3 No Medical Advice or Doctor-Patient Relationship

VitaCase is strictly an organizational utility. Nothing stored in or displayed by the Application constitutes medical diagnosis, clinical interpretation, treatment guidance, or a substitute for a licensed physician.

1.4 User Responsibility

You remain solely responsible for verifying medication schedules, prescription dosages, doctor appointments, and the accuracy of any files or notes you store inside the Application.

1.5 Notification Reliability

Local medication and appointment alerts depend on your device operating system, battery optimization settings, Do Not Disturb preferences, and permissions. Always maintain backup reminders for time-critical medication.

1.6 Emergency Notice

Never rely on VitaCase during a medical emergency. Call your local emergency services immediately (such as 112 in Türkiye and the EU, 911 in the US/Canada, or 999 in the UK).

1.7 Local Custody and No Cloud Backups

VitaCase does not create remote user accounts or store backups of your health vault on Limanex servers. Optional security features include a 6-digit PIN and local biometric lock. Lost PINs or uninstalled devices without user-managed backups cannot be recovered by LIMANEX.

1.8 Third-Party SDKs

The Application integrates third-party diagnostic and advertising services — specifically Google AdMob, Google UMP, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, and Sentry — isolated from your encrypted health vault.

1.9 Acceptable Use

You agree to use the Application for personal, lawful purposes only and not to reverse engineer, tamper with, redistribute, or interfere with ad verification or application integrity.

1.10 Intellectual Property

All software code, UI assets, trademarks, and documentation belong to LIMANEX. All PDFs, notes, doctor entries, and personal records you add remain strictly yours.

1.11 Disclaimer of Warranties

To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, the Application is provided "AS IS" and "AS AVAILABLE" without implied warranties of uninterrupted uptime or device-level notification delivery, preserving non-waivable statutory consumer rights.

1.12 Limitation of Liability

LIMANEX is not liable for indirect or consequential damages arising from missed reminders, device failure, or data loss due to uninstallation or forgotten local PINs, except where liability cannot be excluded by mandatory law.

1.13 Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

These Terms are governed by the laws of the Republic of Türkiye. Mandatory consumer protection rules, including statutory mediation ("arabuluculuk") and Consumer Arbitration Committees ("Tüketici Hakem Heyeti") where applicable, remain fully preserved.

1.14 Contact

For questions regarding these Terms, contact limanexsoftware@outlook.com.

Section 2 — Privacy Policy and Global Data Protection (GDPR)

2.1 Local Vault Encryption Architecture

Your PDF documents, blood test files, medications, saved doctors, and health notes are encrypted locally using AES-256-GCM, with encryption keys protected through FlutterSecureStorage, Android Keystore, or iOS Keychain.

2.2 Zero Server Transmission of Health Records

On-device health vault data is never transmitted to, stored on, analyzed by, or monetized by LIMANEX servers. VitaCase does not use remote OCR servers or external cloud AI to scan your PDFs.

2.3 Biometric Authentication

Optional fingerprint or face unlock uses your device operating system's local authentication APIs (local_auth). Biometric templates never leave your device hardware.

2.4 Third-Party Services Integrated for Stability and Ads

To maintain stability and keep VitaCase free to use, the Application integrates four isolated third-party SDKs:

  • Firebase Analytics (Google LLC): Aggregated app usage events and stability metrics (no health documents, doctor names, or medication titles).
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC): Crash stack traces, OS version, and device hardware context.
  • Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.): Runtime error telemetry to identify and fix application crashes.
  • Google AdMob and UMP SDK: Ad delivery and GDPR/EEA consent management.

2.5 International Data Transfers

Diagnostic and advertising metadata handled by Google LLC and Functional Software, Inc. may be processed in the United States or globally under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).

2.6 GDPR Legal Bases

Where GDPR applies, processing relies on Article 6(1)(b) (contract performance for core local functionality), Article 6(1)(f) (legitimate interest in crash stability), and Article 6(1)(a) / Article 9(2)(a) (explicit consent where prompted via UMP for ad personalization or optional user choices).

2.7 Retention by Third-Party SDKs

Local vault records persist solely until you delete them or uninstall the Application. Retention of anonymized telemetry and crash logs is governed by Google's and Sentry's standard retention windows.

2.8 Your GDPR Rights

Where applicable under GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or object to processing, withdraw ad consent in settings, and lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

2.9 Children's Privacy

VitaCase is not directed at children under 13 (or 16 in EEA jurisdictions without verifiable parental authorization).

2.10 Data Controller

Operator / Controller: LIMANEX, Republic of Türkiye. Contact: limanexsoftware@outlook.com.

Section 3 — Türkiye — 6698 Sayılı KVKK Aydınlatma Bilgilendirmesi

3.1 Veri Sorumlusu

6698 sayılı Kişisel Verilerin Korunması Kanunu ("KVKK") uyarınca, VitaCase uygulamasına ilişkin teknik veri süreçleri bakımından veri sorumlusu sıfatıyla LIMANEX ("Veri Sorumlusu") hareket etmektedir.

3.2 Yerel (On-Device) Veri Saklama Mimarisi

Uygulama içerisine eklediğiniz kan tahlili PDF dosyaları, tıbbi belgeler, ilaç hatırlatmaları ve kayıtlı doktor bilgileri yalnızca kendi cihazınızda AES-256-GCM şifreleme ve işletim sistemi yerel güvenlik modülleri (FlutterSecureStorage / local_auth) ile saklanır. Belgelerinizde OCR metin taraması yapılmaz ve bu içerikler LIMANEX sunucularına aktarılmaz.

3.3 İşlenen Teknik ve Teşhis Verileri

Uygulama kararlılığının sağlanması ve ücretsiz sürümün sürdürülebilmesi amacıyla Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Sentry ve Google AdMob SDK'ları aracılığıyla yalnızca cihaz modeli, işletim sistemi sürümü, çökme günlükleri (stack trace) ve reklam gösterim/onay bilgileri işlenebilir.

3.4 Hukuki Sebepler

Bu teknik veriler KVKK m. 5/2-f (veri sorumlusunun meşru menfaatleri doğrultusunda uygulama güvenliği ve hata giderme) ve KVKK m. 5/2-ç (hukuki yükümlülüklerin yerine getirilmesi) ile açık rızanın gerektiği durumlarda rıza esaslarına dayanılarak işlenir.

3.5 Yurt Dışına Aktarım

Çökme ve analitik teşhis verileri yalnızca altyapı sağlayıcıları Google LLC ve Functional Software, Inc. (Sentry) nezdinde, ilgili hizmetlerin küresel sunucularında işlenebilir.

3.6 Verilerin Silinmesi

Cihazınızdaki tüm sağlık belgelerini, doktor kayıtlarını ve hatırlatmaları uygulama içerisindeki Ayarlar → Veri ve Güvenlik → "Tüm Verileri Temizle" adımıyla veya uygulamayı cihazdan kaldırarak kalıcı olarak silebilirsiniz.

3.7 KVKK Madde 11 Kapsamındaki Haklarınız

KVKK'nın 11. maddesi uyarınca kişisel verilerinizin işlenip işlenmediğini öğrenme, bilgi talep etme, düzeltilmesini veya silinmesini isteme ve kanuna aykırı işleme nedeniyle zararın giderilmesini talep etme haklarına sahipsiniz.

3.8 Başvuru Yöntemi

Başvurularınızı konu satırına "KVKK İlgili Kişi Başvurusu — VitaCase" yazarak limanexsoftware@outlook.com adresine iletebilirsiniz. Talepler en geç 30 gün içinde ücretsiz olarak yanıtlanır.

Section 4 — Health and Medical Disclaimer

4.1 General

VitaCase is designed strictly as a personal digital file cabinet, medication alert notebook, and doctor contact directory.

4.2 Medical and Healthcare

VitaCase is not a certified medical device. It does not perform OCR parsing, diagnostic evaluation, clinical risk scoring, or interpretation of lab values in your PDFs.

4.3 Medication and Prescription

The Application does not verify drug interactions, contraindications, prescription validity, or safe dosage levels. Follow instructions given by your physician or pharmacist.

4.4 Appointment Calendar and Doctor Records

Saved doctor phone numbers, clinic locations, and appointment notes are manually entered and maintained by you; verify clinic changes directly with your healthcare provider.

4.5 Notification Reliability

Scheduled reminders may be delayed or suppressed by operating system power-saving policies, permissions, or restarts.

4.6 Emergency Situations

In any acute medical situation, contact local emergency medical services immediately (112, 911, or 999).

4.7 Assumption of Risk

You use the Application's scheduling and file storage features at your own discretion and responsibility.

Section 5 — Data Retention and Deletion Policy

5.1 Local Architecture

VitaCase stores your health PDFs, medications, and saved doctor records locally inside your device sandbox under AES-256-GCM encryption.

5.2 Three Deletion Methods

  • Selective Manual Deletion: Delete individual PDFs, medication entries, saved doctors, or appointments directly in the app.
  • In-App Wipe: Use Settings → Data & Security → "Clear All Data" ("Tüm Verileri Temizle") to destroy the local database, encrypted files, and encryption keys permanently.
  • Uninstallation: Removing VitaCase deletes its local application directory (subject to OS-level Google Drive / Android Auto Backup settings managed by you).

5.3 Third-Party Telemetry Limits

Anonymized diagnostic logs retained by Firebase or Sentry cannot be linked back to local vault contents because local health records are never transmitted.

5.4 Formal Requests

Send privacy or telemetry deletion requests to limanexsoftware@outlook.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request — VitaCase, or view the step-by-step guide on the Data Deletion Instructions page.
