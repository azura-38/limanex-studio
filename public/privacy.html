2. Core Architecture: Local-First Storage

Key Architecture Principle: Meowoirs is designed as a local-first application. Your personal memories, cat notes, and photos remain on your device and are not uploaded to, synchronized with, or stored on LIMANEX servers.

The following user-created content is stored locally on your Android device in an embedded SQLite database managed via AndroidX Room:

  • Cat profiles (name, classification as home or street cat, profile notes).
  • Cat photo references (Content URIs selected via the system photo picker).
  • Memories and journal entries (notes, reflections, timestamps).
  • Selected mood tags (e.g., Happy, Peaceful, Playful, Calm).
  • User profile preferences (nickname, avatar selection, favorite cat preference).
  • Application display settings (theme mode, interface language).

Meowoirs currently does not require:

  • Account registration, login, or password creation.
  • Cloud synchronization or remote backup.
  • LIMANEX-hosted server storage for your journal content.

3. Photo Selection & Permissions

Meowoirs uses Android’s system Photo Picker (ActivityResultContracts.PickVisualMedia) for selecting photos of your cats and memories.

  • The application does not request broad device storage or media library permissions (such as READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, or READ_MEDIA_VIDEO).
  • The application does not request camera access hardware permissions (CAMERA).
  • Only individual photos explicitly selected by you through the system picker are accessible to the application, using system-managed persistable content URIs.

4. Device Storage, Backup, and Deletion

Because all journal records are stored locally within the application's sandbox on your physical device:

  • Deletion: When you delete a cat profile or memory using the in-app delete buttons, the corresponding record is removed from the local database on your device. Note that deleting a cat profile removes that cat record while preserving associated memories according to application design.
  • Permanent Nature of Deletion: Deleting content within Meowoirs is immediate and permanent at the application level. LIMANEX cannot recover deleted memories, notes, or profiles because LIMANEX does not maintain a cloud copy or server backup of your journal.
  • Local Storage, Clearing Data & OS Backups: Meowoirs does not operate its own cloud backup or synchronization service. Clearing application data or uninstalling the application may remove locally stored Meowoirs data. Android or device-level backup and restoration behavior, where available, is controlled by the operating system and device configuration and is not operated or guaranteed by LIMANEX.
  • Independent Backups: You are solely responsible for maintaining any independent backups of photos, notes, or memories you consider irreplaceable.

5. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

Meowoirs integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements. AdMob may automatically process certain technical and device data to deliver ads, prevent fraud, and comply with advertising policies.

What AdMob May Process: Technical identifiers such as your Google Advertising ID (GAID), IP address, general coarse device information, app performance telemetry, and ad interaction data in accordance with Google's policies and your privacy choices.

Separation of Data: Your personal cat notes, memories, cat names, moods, and photos are never transmitted to Google AdMob. AdMob only receives technical telemetry required for serving ads.

You can learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services by visiting Google Advertising Privacy Policy.

Where required by applicable law (such as in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or Switzerland under the GDPR/ePrivacy Directive), Meowoirs utilizes the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to gather and manage user consent for personalized or non-personalized advertising.

7. Technical Diagnostics (Firebase Crashlytics)

To monitor stability, identify software defects, and prevent application crashes, Meowoirs includes Google Firebase Crashlytics.

Information Processed: In the event of an unhandled error or crash, Crashlytics may collect technical diagnostic details, including:

  • Crash stack traces and error codes.
  • Device state (operating system version, device model, available memory).
  • Application version and build number.

Zero Journal Content in Diagnostics: Meowoirs does not intentionally log or transmit personal memory notes, cat names, journal entries, photo URI paths, or emotional reflections to Firebase Crashlytics.

For more information, please review Firebase Privacy and Security Documentation.

8. Data Retention

A. Local Content: Retained on your physical device until you delete specific entries, clear the application's data via Android settings, or uninstall the application.

B. Third-Party Technical Data: Diagnostic crash reports and advertising interaction data processed by Google/Firebase services are retained according to Google's standard retention schedules and applicable legal requirements. LIMANEX does not define or control Google's internal retention cycles.
